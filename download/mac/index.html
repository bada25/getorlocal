Install

  1. Open Orlocal-Mac.zip from your Downloads. It unzips to Orlocal.
  2. Drag Orlocal into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. If your Mac says it can't check the app, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter the email you bought with and your license key from the email.
