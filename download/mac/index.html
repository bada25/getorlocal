Install

  1. Open Downloads and find Orlocal. If you see Orlocal-Mac.zip instead, double click it first.
  2. Drag Orlocal into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. The first time, your Mac blocks it. Click Done.
  4. Choose the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open next to Orlocal.
  5. Click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password and click OK.
  6. Enter the email you bought with and your license key from the email, then click Unlock.

macOS 13 or newer. Questions: support@getorlocal.com
